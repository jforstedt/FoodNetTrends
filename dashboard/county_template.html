What supports this view?

The combined model shares information between neighboring counties while allowing each county’s trend to change over time.

What this view cannot establish

These are historical estimates from the full 2004–2019 fit, not forecasts. The retrospective forecast check trained through 2016 and evaluated 2017–2019 after those years had already been examined during model development.

Raw-to-clean reconciliation confirms consistency through this pipeline; it does not confirm that every infection was detected or reported. The established state-level analysis remains separate.

Sources, model scope, and provenance

Scope: Salmonella, 2004–2019; historical catchment; travel NO / UNKNOWN / YES; diagnosis categories CIDT+ / CX+ / PARASITIC. No additional pathogen or subtype validation is implied.

Display boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau, 2019 cartographic boundaries (1:5,000,000). This display uses the historical county identifiers. The model’s adjacency graph is the separately audited 2010 graph, including its water and cross-state neighbors. Display boundaries do not change the model.
