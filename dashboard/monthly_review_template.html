Interpretation and provenance

RW1 lets deviations persist and uncertainty accumulate. AR1 allows deviations to fade toward a persistent state level. Neither is guaranteed to forecast well for every pathogen. The interface displays completed comparisons; it does not fit, select, or average models.

Monthly curves, diagnostic-method adjustment and prospective forecasts are not included in this review. Cryptosporidium ends in 2017. The original records were not changed. Internal aggregate results should be handled as study data.
